# Round journal

One short historical entry per round of at least 30 changed text lines,
in YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md. Smaller commits may omit it; journal lines do not
count toward the threshold.
Append later entries at the bottom; preserve earlier findings. Record
Finding:, Validation:, and Limits:, and link evidence and asks.

Current status stays in docs/tasks/ASKS.md and docs/plans/README.md.
Deferred work belongs in the appropriate plan or human queue, not only here.
The canonical journal and commit rules are in docs/git/COMMITS.md.
