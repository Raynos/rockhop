Fresh rider rig comparison — independent, unaccepted
Last verified: 2026-10-01

The new rig improves the seated silhouette and reduces hip edge stretch, but does NOT solve sitting. Fresh weights produce most of the measured stretch improvement; relocating joints adds a smaller benefit. Actual triangle crossings persist, early transition samples regress, and the fresh rig's endpoint remains 16.4 mm above the sampled saddle top. This is a completed causal comparison, not an accepted riding asset. No source or production asset was edited or promoted.

CAUSAL CONCLUSION AND NEXT TECHNIQUE
The current inverse binds, axes, hierarchy, indices, normalization and rest pose are sound at micrometre scale. The original low pelvis/thigh transition creates substantial shear under hip flexion; moving that field fixes much of the stretch without changing joints. Anatomy placement influences shape but cannot explain away remaining cloth crossings. Extra helpers and DQ alone are not universal remedies.
The next technique should address garment deformation and contact together: a collision-constrained volumetric deformation cage or physically meaningful cloth layer, validated against all hinge sweeps, continuous stand-to-seat transitions, actual saddle support and held-out riding states. Keep the accepted standing surfaces/textures frozen and first prove that technique in an isolated adapter. Further height-band tweaks or six-pose surface corrections are not justified as the next fix. This task establishes why the fresh-rig-only hypothesis fails; it does not abandon the user's appearance/Garage/riding goal.

PRIMARY SOURCES / PRACTICAL CONCEPTS
A joint is the pivot; a bone's direction and roll define local bend/twist axes. An inverse bind removes the original skeleton placement; weights then blend each posed joint's effect. LBS averages rotation matrices and can shrink/shear a bend. IK selects joint angles reaching a contact; its pole chooses the bend plane. glTF stores the baked joint animation, not Blender's authoring IK constraints.
