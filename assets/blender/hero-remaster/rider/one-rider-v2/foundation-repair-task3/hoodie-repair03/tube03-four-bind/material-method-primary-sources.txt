Practical construction notes — primary sources and measured limits

A bone rotates a weighted group of points. It does not tell a hoodie where its
cloth surface ends, how two sewn edges stay together, or where the saddle must
support it. Our completed rig ablation improved stretch but left crossings and
support failures; the actual triangle evidence motivates the separate garment
construction now underway.

The sleeve driver transports a material cross-section along a responding curved
centreline with only the rotation needed between successive tangents. Hanson and
Ma describe parallel transport for stable tube/ribbon frames, including straight
and inflected curves where Frenet frames can change direction abruptly. This
supports our frame construction; it is NOT a collision, anatomical or garment
volume theorem. A tube can still fold when the inside bend is too tight, as our
literal rest failures demonstrated.
Primary paper: Hanson & Ma, Parallel Transport Approach to Curve Framing,
Indiana University Technical Report425,1995, sections2.2–3.
https://scholarworks.iu.edu/dspace/bitstreams/23d6fd59-b151-4064-88a7-f7e058b2cc69/download

Blender Surface Deform binds one mesh to a target surface; Blender Mesh Deform
uses a closed mesh cage. These are examples of deformation driven by a surface
or surrounding volume rather than only bone weights. They guide possible next
techniques; neither modifier has been qualified/exported as our runtime fix.
https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/surface_deform.html
https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/mesh_deform.html

The measured clipped-edge distinction is concrete: a new point halfway along an
old mesh edge must remain halfway between its moving endpoints. Averaging both
its rest position and weights does not guarantee that. On our430 new source-edge
rows, stock LBS differs from the actual posed-parent interpolation by at most
2.313mm across480 literal controls,1.691mm at304. The material driver explicitly
uses the posed parents. Its freely responding cloth is therefore separate from
the standard-four GLB. Actual stockFour export parity (77.63nm) verifies its own
LBS wiring; it does not establish equivalence to the nonlinear material driver.
Local measurement: clipped-edge-lbs-contract.json.

A small skeletal change, four weights, an RMF tube, a smooth cap or preserved
normal can each pass an isolated test while the whole rider still looks worse.
Current standing remains rejected. Gates retain actual triangles, shaded/gray
played-control views, source identity, seams, continuous motion and support.
