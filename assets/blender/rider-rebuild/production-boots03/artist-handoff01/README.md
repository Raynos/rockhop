## Actual failure and viewing correction

The direct original toe sculpture and its one targeted upper-flank repair
preserved selected detail and rounded cap. The parent viewed all eight current
original-vs-repair images and rejected rest fit. R negative-transverse views
show tiny skin spots; L negative-transverse views show large ankle/quarter,
medial forefoot and distal exposure. No body points were moved, shrunk or hidden.

The filenames `lateral` are misleading: the same negative-transverse camera
offset shows **R anatomical lateral but L anatomical medial**. These are not
matched anatomical lateral views. Read `reference/frame-camera-winding-read.json`.
Both foot frames match the recorded anatomy exactly. R is a positive-determinant
affine; L is reflected, with face and UV corner order reversed together. The
current arrays' mirrored counterparts differ by at most 3.10 mm transversely,
with retained rear differing by at most 2.90 mm. Thus the view-side difference
explains much of the apparent R/L disparity; it does not make the visible skin
an imaging illusion. The unshown R medial side cannot be treated as a pass.
No basic double-mirror/frame-sign error was found by the cheap source/array read.
It is not an independent Blender reopen or GPU transport certification.

The pinned wearer builder clears animation and sets pose bases to identity.
Neither boot recipe writes poses or changes frames. All supplied images are
rest diagnostics. There is no successful ankle, toe, footpeg, bike or complete
outfit motion evidence in this package. The bounded coded brush mechanism is
closed; do not start another lattice, gain sweep, chart fit or ray campaign.
