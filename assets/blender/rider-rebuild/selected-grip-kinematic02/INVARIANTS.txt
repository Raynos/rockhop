Finite-face thumb construction

Let O be the fixed CMC origin; v is the actual skinned pulp point minus O and n its unit material normal, evaluated after MCP/IP articulation but before CMC swing. All positive skin influences of the pulp patch must descend from CMC. A CMC rotation preserves |v| and v dot n. For desired point P and inward-facing normal -N, the two necessary and sufficient nondegenerate invariants are |P-O|=|v| and (P-O) dot (-N)=v dot n.

A real finite bar triangle plane has N dot P=h; consequently the second invariant is N dot O-h. Solve one declared MCP/IP coupling against that scalar plane distance. The remaining valid target set is the sphere |P-O|=|v| intersected with the real triangle plane. Intersect a line along the bar face with that circle, check both quadratic roots against finite geometry, then derive the unique proper rotation between the orthonormal (reach,pulp-normal) frames. This includes true axial roll coupled with the changed MCP/IP articulation and spatial swing. It cannot fake shorter reach by merely twisting CMC.

Exact verification uses the same blended source material patch after applying the final CMC rotation; measure contact position error, opposed normal, all hand-owned vertices and triangle crossing. A feasible pulp point alone never proves the whole glove clear or the wrist anatomically plausible. No source geometry, native rest, translations or skin field changes are permitted.
