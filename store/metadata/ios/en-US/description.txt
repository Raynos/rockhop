Rockhop is a side-on dirt bike game about reading the ground and riding a clean line. Control the throttle, brake and your rider's weight as the bike reacts to rock steps, logs, ramps and gaps.

TWELVE HAND-BUILT LINES
Ride twelve courses through four zones: a coastal scrapyard, an alpine forest trail, a desert quarry and the snowline. Each course has its own obstacles and a faster line to discover.

ONE TAP TO TRY AGAIN
Tap once to restart from the last checkpoint. Hold to restart the whole course. Get back on the bike quickly and try a different approach.

RIDE IT CLEAN
Every bail counts. Finish clean and beat the clock for Bronze, Silver, Gold or Diamond. Your saved personal best can ride beside you as a ghost, and you can replay a finished run.

EARN YOUR SECOND BIKE
Start on the Rookie. Earn Scrap by clearing courses and improving medals, then spend 800 Scrap to unlock the Pro. Its stronger drive and longer travel open up different lines; the final four courses reward mastering those upper routes.

MADE FOR PLAYING ANYWHERE
- Landscape touch controls with a thumb on each side
- Plays completely offline; everything is on your phone
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no account
- No data collected

The physics runs at a fixed step, so your inputs determine the ride. When you land it, you earned it.
