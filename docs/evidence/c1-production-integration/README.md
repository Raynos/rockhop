The standalone prototype and its earlier GO/brake comparison remain in [the candidate evidence](../c1-challenge/README.md).

The cue stays in the upper left as the camera and scenery move, clears at the ramp, and does not obscure the rider or landing. This is a rendered replay, not a human touch session.
