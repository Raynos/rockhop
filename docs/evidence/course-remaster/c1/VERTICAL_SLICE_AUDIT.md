## Ordered implementation brief

### 1. Make the first brake decision readable in real time

**Why first:** C1's [authored course](../../../../src/tracks/rockhop/coast.ts) asks the rider to settle a 22° pallet ramp after Marker 2. The fixed HUD approach cue spans x179.6–209.6, and the newer in-world BRAKE board sits at x195.5, **14.1 m before the ramp foot**. The [board's played report](../../c1-quality-next/README.md) says the ramp first enters the tight camera roughly one second before contact. The earlier [blind paused-step ride](../../c1-blind-play/played.mp4) needed four attempts/three bails; the cue conveyed “brake” but not the amount. Two later **briefed** CLI riders cleared in one attempt each ([session report](../../stranger-2026-09-29/README.md)). Held GO still clears 0/6 bike/seed trials, and the [fault evidence](../../c1-crash-feedback/README.md) shows a specific deck/causeway correction, one-second auto-respawn and next-tick manual restart. This proves a real skill gate and fast simulated retry, not that a new touch rider sees the ramp early enough.

**Build/check:** First run three uncoached landscape-phone rides and log when each rider notices the ramp/sign, what brake action they predict, x and cause of each fault, and time from Retry tap to controllable visible bike. If they see the board too late, reveal the ramp and landing earlier in the camera and move the physical warning upstream of the braking decision; keep contact geometry and the clean input unchanged in that pass. Show the ramp top and container landing together without hiding tire contact. Accept when all three can name the brake move before the lip, at least two clear, and a failed rider can explain the correction; preserve zero held-GO clears, both-bike exact finish hashes and next-tick control. Measure rendered restart latency on a physical phone rather than inferring it from simulation ticks.

### 2. Bring the whole coast foreground up to the menu's art standard

**Observed gap:** In the [full played ride](full-ride-phone.mp4), the revised winch, service pier and salvage derrick make the x205–240 landing more authored; its [round report](README.md) says the ramp and tire contact stay unobstructed. Across the full course, the pale quay and flat cyan water still occupy long stretches, the photographic freighter supplies much of the harbor depth, and the foreground machinery reads schematic at phone scale. The earlier [played Coast review](../../gameplay-audit/VISUAL_REVIEW.md) identifies the same road/water/photo-plate hierarchy. The opening-to-finish scene therefore lacks a consistent material, shadow and contact language, even though one set piece improved.

**Build/check:** Extend the harbor kit along the *entire* ride: layered wet quay and exposed tide-edge materials, modeled container/ship/crane masses at plausible scale, local light/shadow and wheel-contact response, with the brake ramp as the focal obstacle. Preserve the sign, wheel line and landing silhouette through x180–240. Judge matched-input **full moving before/after rides**, plus the same ramp and causeway fault windows at 852×392; require no obstacle/contact occlusion and exact replay hashes if edits are visual only. Profile the resulting draw calls, triangles, memory and sustained frame pacing on the target landscape phones. The current 125–135 calls / 168k–203k triangles near the ramp are a headless low-tier sample, not a device budget or before/after delta ([moving probe](perf-moving.json)). Listen to the real sound/haptics on device; all retained clips are silent.

### 3. Make the first reward and return path legible enough to invite a second run

**What works:** The user accepted the finish layout direction. The production [played result states](../../finish-remaster/state-audit/README.md) distinguish first clear, faster same-medal PB, Gold→Diamond upgrade and no-gain after reload; +220 then +80 Scrap pays once, and Retry/Replay/Map/Next reach their destinations. The full App flow carried a real Diamond, +300 Scrap and wallet to Garage. The selected clean Rookie run is only **0.25 s inside Diamond** ([medal audit](../../campaign-medal-audit/README.md)); two briefed CLI clears were slower. None of this measures whether a new rider reads the next goal or voluntarily chases it. The state audit found result tag/label text as small as 8–9 CSS px, later raised to 10; physical phone readability remains open.

**Build/check:** Keep the accepted report layout and earned Scrap logic. On a real landscape phone, ask each first-clear player to say their medal, next time/skill goal, Scrap-to-Pro progress and intended next button without coaching. If they cannot, enlarge/clarify the existing labels and make the next C1 improvement concrete (brake timing or a time delta) before adding reward mechanics. Require one of three players to **voluntarily** Retry or Replay for a better C1 result. Capture one uninterrupted *current-source* Menu→3D map→C1 ride→live report→Map→Garage→reopen flow at phone size, with the medal, wallet and lock state surviving reload and no duplicate Scrap on the same medal. This replaces the older painted-map integration proof; separate map and result clips cannot certify the complete first-win loop.

**Open before sign-off:** no uncoached real-time physical-phone C1 sample, no current-SHA continuous 3D-map-to-Garage capture, no audible device mix, no sustained phone frame-time measurement, and no observed voluntary replay. The deterministic clean rides and scripted fault clips are strong regression controls, not substitutes for those player judgments.
