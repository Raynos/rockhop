### Frozen exemplar capture protocol

All twelve clean Rookie rides were captured from an isolated `git archive` of full commit `609293eaac58913420a1e06ca8fc530bfc907cc1`, with its own installed dependencies and production `dist`. The archive's source fingerprint is `d61e8029`; each existing input header says `src=a415fcb8`. Each complete recording still finished with **zero faults and an identical finish time, tick and state hash in Node plus two fresh headless browser loads**; each compact capture record above gives the exact input SHA-256 and verified hash. The discrepancy in source fingerprints remains a warning about comparing broader gameplay behavior, not a mismatch in these twelve recorded runs.

The videos are silent game captures from headless Chromium on ANGLE Metal at 852×394, 20 fps, renderer quality `high`, with a 0.5-second finish tail. They were compressed to H.264 CRF 26; twelve Rookie and four Pro videos total **105.33 MB**. Their camera checks found zero riding frames outside the central box, zero roll violations and zero clamped frames. The `endOfClipHash` in a capture record includes the finish tail; use `verifiedFinish.hash` for the exact finish-state comparison. The four Pro inputs cross the D3/S1/S2/S3 upper goal and earn Diamond on this archive's simulation. D3 Pro finishes at **32.350 s** here; the older `8fa49c3e` medal audit records **32.950 s**, so compare using the capture's own source and input SHA. These captures establish a same-SHA moving baseline for the full campaign and final-four route pairs, not iOS Safari performance or new-player readability.

## Open baseline capture and measurement gaps

1. **Fault coverage:** all twelve Rookie courses and the final four Pro upper routes have complete clean MP4s. The separate [twelve-course fault/retry index](fault-retry/README.md) now links continuous played failure-to-control windows for every Rookie course: older C1/C2 clips plus ten new current-source captures. Seven new windows use briefed Rookie inputs; S1/S2/S3 are scripted Rookie probes. A3/D2 show restart without a changed first input. These close the mechanical evidence gap, not human understanding or physical-device latency.
2. **Human difficulty:** nine courses have one briefed CLI rider; C1 and C2 have two, and D2 has both bikes. Collect at least two *fresh* independent Rookie CLI sessions per course after the harness default correction, then uncoached touch players on physical phones. Record attempts-to-clear, fault locations, whether the cause was understood, fault-to-control delay, bike choice, actual medals and voluntary retry. The 8-attempt D1 and 7-attempt D3 samples need diagnosis before raising difficulty.
3. **Source consistency:** the 15 CLI inputs are stamped `a415fcb8`, and the clean bot medal pins are `8fa49c3e`; the twelve selected input headers also say `a415fcb8` against archive fingerprint `d61e8029`. The exact twelve-input replay passed, but other old inputs must be verified against any chosen comparison source. Older clips span candidate rounds and some isolated QA builds. Do not treat a short clip's exact endpoint hash as a complete-course replay proof.
4. **Visual and device quality:** the [played visual review](../gameplay-audit/VISUAL_REVIEW.md) identifies flat Coast road/water, repeated Alpine trees and truck, sparse Quarry machinery, and uniform Snowline snow. Existing brief clips establish those leads but leave the full ride's scenery rhythm, camera, art continuity, iOS Safari frame pacing and touch readability unmeasured. Compare full moving clips at phone size and test a physical landscape iPhone and Android device before calling any course remastered.
5. **Medal and bike economy:** the current selected bot pins give 11 Rookie Gold / 1 Rookie Diamond and 8 Pro Gold / 4 Pro Diamond. D3/S1/S2/S3 Pro upper lines are sampled Diamond routes; this does not prove a new player can see or learn them. The [Scrap audit](../campaign-medal-audit/README.md) also notes a Bronze-only player can select D3 after six clears with only 600 Scrap, before the 800-Scrap Pro unlock. Recheck route cues, medal clocks, unlock affordance and player rewards after course geometry changes.

## Review leads from existing played evidence

The [played visual review](../gameplay-audit/VISUAL_REVIEW.md) flags a wide gap between menu art and riding foreground: Coast uses a broad gray road and cyan water with much of the harbor in a photo plate; Alpine repeats triangular pines and a box-like truck; Quarry lacks foreground rock and machinery depth; Snowline repeats white surfaces, trees and thin landmarks. The new complete clips make those observations reviewable across each full course, but this inventory does not certify a fresh aesthetic verdict. Judge the moving videos at phone size before choosing asset and camera fixes.

For challenge, the [briefed CLI baseline](../stranger-2026-09-29/README.md) puts D1 at 8 attempts and D3 at 7, while C2's Pier 2 cue led one rider toward a nose dive; S3's generic ramp hint conflicts with its opening kicker. S1's single Pro session cleared in one attempt, and the current selected upper-route recordings alone cannot establish the second bike's perceived value to a new player. The 0/72 held-GO gate proves only that passive throttle does not clear the sampled cases. Fault-to-control latency and uncoached phone attempts are still needed to retune the difficulty curve.
