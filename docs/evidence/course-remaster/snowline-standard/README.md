# Snowline Standard: source-only S1–S3 candidate

**Status:** original modular kit authored and offline verified; not integrated, played, or accepted. This is **0/3 Snowline course signoffs**, and the full game remains **0/12**. `src/render/world/zones/snowlineStandard.ts` is intentionally unimported by the shared renderer. No physics, collider, track, seed, or existing accepted S1/S2 art was edited.

## Existing ride evidence and gap

Reviewed the matched 852×392 played Pro full rides and fault/retry sheets at `../s1/bridge-art/after/pro/`, `../s2/cornice-landmark/after/full-pro/`, `../s3/`, `../pro-envelope/clips/s1/`, `../pro-envelope/clips/s2/`, `../pro-envelope/clips/s3/`, and `../fault-retry/`. The mountain plate gives atmosphere, but the contact tread and gorge repeat broad white/blue bands; current lift towers and chairs are thin repeated silhouettes, the S2 snowcat reads as a red block, and S3 lacks a distinguishable summit landmark. The station bridge and cornice lip are functional and must remain readable in the moving candidate.

The offline [nine-angle board](nine-angle-board.jpg) shows the wall, lift tower, and snowcat in three views each. The [family board](family-board.jpg) additionally shows both distinct wall forms, the under-shelf bracket, station, and summit beacon. These are model review only, not in-game proof.

## Candidate kit

The editable Blender master and deterministic `build.py` generate eight named archetypes: **two thick glacier walls** with distinct carved shoulders, buttresses, rift pockets, colored sediment/ice faces and broken end returns; hanging shelf ice; a braced lift tower with sheave/ladder/anchors; a chair and cable grip; the S1 station; a detailed S2/S3 snowcat with bogies, individual grousers, glazed cab, vents, hydraulic blade and roof lamps; and an S3 summit signal gantry. The model uses original neutral grain and ice-normal maps at 256² each, tinted by four material-response palettes. The contact geometry leaf adds one 40 m culled mesh per chunk: intermittent 9 m wind-scoured shoulder patches and a three-band blue ice face with dark joints. Its top is at least 0.115 m below the physical ground and all profile gap spans are omitted.

Full packed GLB: **310,760 B, 9,989 prototype triangles, 13 prototype material primitives**, SHA-256 `e4cbd81ddd3abfe7bb3dc84d55b399ee42289b98963ec34d72dfe0be1d18efd2`. LOD: **263,732 B, 5,617 triangles, 13 material primitives**, SHA-256 `0210b274a2b7f64cc3723d0a44131e80833fda5b14eb939b25864d9158c13eae`. The two embedded original maps total 129,824 compressed bytes; at 256² RGBA plus mip estimates they occupy ~0.665 MiB together. The glTF has two texture descriptors after duplicate grain collapse. The carved contact strip adds **2,112 / 2,100 / 1,900 triangles** over all of S1 / S2 / S3, but only one or two 40 m chunks should be visible at a time. These are offline source counts, **not** an observed in-engine draw/triangle/phone budget. The proposed ≤8 net visible draws and ≤20k near triangles must be measured after selective legacy removal.

The [manifest](manifest.json) records every packed-model, board, source and Blender-master hash. The [decoder report](decoder-report.json) confirms both compressed files parse with the exact production Three `GLTFLoader` + bundled `MeshoptDecoder`, eight expected object names, finite bounds, COLOR_0 and material slots. The Node validation removes image references for geometry parsing; browser GLB image decode remains an integration check. The [placement report](placements.json) is regenerated from the compiled collider truth: S1 hash `d062c03be545fd64`, S2 `9c9e7d52ddebf852`, S3 `70b3eeca1aed9ccb`.

## Parent integration contract

1. Atomically stage the verified full/LOD pair under `public/models/course-kits/snowline-standard/` **only when the browser test is ready**. Rebuild the existing model catalog and offline pack together. All public models are snapshotted/prefetched on Menu boot; the GLB must remain undecoded on Menu/Garage backdrop.
2. Build the old `zoneKit` first to preserve all seeded RNG draws. Before normal `buildBatches`, call `snapshotSnowlineAnchors(zk.batches)`. Move only `icewall0`, `icewall1`, `lifttower`, `liftchair`, and distant `snowcat` batch items to separately built fallback groups, as A1 does; the existing batch merger otherwise combines tower/chair/vehicle material with unrelated painted props and cannot be hidden selectively. Keep those fallbacks visible until `mountCourseAssets(loadSnowlineStandard(track, anchors, detail, lib))` mounts successfully. On failure or late cancellation, leave/reveal them. The leaf owns GLB geometry/material/maps and closes owned `ImageBitmap`s on retirement.
3. Retain `liftcable`, all collision/route meshes, S1 `zone:s1-lift-bridge-terminal`, and S2 `zone:s2-cornice-ice-buttresses` plus `zone:s2-wind-shelf-ice-underside` until played moving comparison approves a replacement. The candidate adds shelf depth beneath ordinary ice ledges and a short below-contact bracket under the S2 lip; its x extent stops 0.12 m before the real gap. Do not hide any of these critical surfaces merely because the candidate loads.
4. For S1 the new station center is x297,z−7.1, so its 16 m roof spans the existing 288–306 m bridge context while its near face remains behind the ±1.55 m riding corridor. The S2/S3 modeled snowcats sit behind real roller/box obstacle groups at x48.9 and x136.7,z−6.2; their near track edges remain around z−3.5. S3 summit beacon sits at x409.1,z−13.8, behind the route and before `finishX=425.1`. The far wall and lift tower/chair placements use the exact old seeded matrices (long 9–14 m wall instances only), preserving course rhythm.
5. Compare complete S1, S2, S3 Rookie and Pro played rides at 852×393, upper-route and held-GO fault/retry windows, and at least two actual phone tiers. Reject if the station masks landing judgement, the snowcat falsely changes the roller line, wall repetition remains flat in motion, the S2 gap appears bridged, or frame pacing/texture/draw budgets regress. Run exact replay, crash/restart, missing/late GLB and cached-offline gates. There is no course signoff before uncoached physical-phone riders.

The current candidate does **not** replace the white riding-tread material or the seeded snowbank/fence/pine families. Those remain part of the overall Snowline full-biome gate; this candidate should be judged in motion and rejected or extended if those existing families still make the complete ride look schematic.
