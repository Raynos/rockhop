## Authored alternatives

The [brick shed nine-angle board](brick-repair-shed-nine-angle.jpg) shows a low older repair shop: masonry piers and courses, deep rolling bays with two different shutter positions, a clear central working bay with bound stock and hoist, upper glazing, cast-stone lintels and sills, steel gutters/downpipes and a weathered standing-seam gable. The [sawtooth hall nine-angle board](sawtooth-maintenance-hall-nine-angle.jpg) shows a taller four-bay serrated zinc roof with closed end infills, vertical clerestories, front workshop bays, a distinct cream loading office, yellow service door, rear manifold and roof extraction. These are **offline source renders**, not gameplay screenshots. Both look materially and structurally different from the old open teal shed and from each other; final screen-size judgement belongs to a played ride.

Each alternative shares one compact original frontage PBR atlas. The bank retains all seven original prototypes and the exact original three 512² Coast map hashes. New material maps are 256² WebP: `frontage-albedo.phone.webp` 16,832 B, `frontage-normal.phone.webp` 1,338 B and `frontage-arm.phone.webp` 1,726 B. Both variants are original scripted Blender geometry and maps, derived solely from the in-repository Coast builder; no third-party art, scans or photos. Editable source is `assets/blender/course-kits/coast-frontage/{build.py,frontage_variants.py}`. The script reproduces the ignored `.blend` masters and all final exports. The source [manifest](../../../../assets/blender/course-kits/coast-frontage/delivery/manifest.json) records all nine prototypes, bounds and image hashes.

Estimated resident atlas allocation rises from 4 to 5 MiB if the new frontage maps are all uploaded. The alternatives themselves have fewer triangles than the old warehouse: brick 3,418/1,706 and sawtooth 2,424/1,356 full/LOD, versus old 4,906/1,830. They use two primitive slots each, against one old slot. Bank-wide counts include all nine prototypes, not all simultaneously visible meshes. Draw count and memory must be measured in a real ride.

Decoded full and LOD bounds satisfy the existing site footprint and ground pivot: brick X ±14.061, game Z −6.5…6.701, Y 0…9.48; sawtooth X −14…14.111, Z −6.451…6.536, Y 0…10.545. Production Three `GLTFLoader` plus bundled `MeshoptDecoder` parsed the packed geometry, with finite positions and mapped region UVs. The Node validation strips image references for geometry parse because it has no browser `ImageBitmap`; browser decode and lifecycle remain open gates. The builder also checks that every original prototype retains its selected triangle/primitive count and every original Coast map retains its SHA-256.
