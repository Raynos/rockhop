# Played visual review — current course renderer

**Scope:** the [five silent played clips](README.md#played-evidence-and-result) from the current game and the live 852×393 menu. These are low renderer quality headless captures, so exact shader and texture sharpness need a separate high-tier iPhone pass. The geometry, camera framing and visual hierarchy findings remain visible in motion.

| Surface | Observed in moving evidence | Studio-quality target |
|---|---|---|
| Coast | [Clip](clips/coast/clip.mp4): the rider is small against a photographic harbour backdrop; foreground shipping containers and tyres sit on a broad flat grey road, with bright flat cyan water behind. | An authored harbor with true depth: ship/crane/container silhouettes, wet material variation, local shadows, spray and tire contact. The chosen riding obstacle should dominate the frame. |
| Alpine | [Clip](clips/alpine/clip.mp4): identical triangular conifers repeat, and the logging truck reads as a red box with cylinders. The rich mountain image behind them highlights the contrast. | Varied tree age/species and canopy, readable log and truck structure, layered forest light, dirt/wood interactions and a camera that frames the skill move. |
| Quarry | [Clip](clips/quarry/clip.mp4): the background pit photograph carries most of the richness; foreground cut is broad and sparse, with a thin dark pulley tower and simple blocks. The rider crashes, but the obstacle and cause are visually weak. | Terraced playable rock, conveyor structure, dust and contact debris; failure should visually trace to the rock or machinery the rider hit. |
| Snowline | [Clip](clips/snowline/clip.mp4): a long uniform white surface, repeated snow trees, isolated black rocks and thin timber strips do not communicate the height or danger of the lift line. | Carved snow/ice forms, cliffs and crevasses with readable depth, wind/snow motion, a purposeful lift landmark and clear landing silhouette. |
| Finish | [Clip](clips/finish/clip.mp4): the bike rolls across a small checker at unchanged scale; a large cream ticket then covers most of the left playfield. The four medals appear, with an icon-only Replay button and no earned reward or upgrade beat. | A brief rider-and-bike hero shot, one readable medal reveal, meaningful earned currency and next goal, then full-size Replay/Retry/Next/Map actions. Keep inputs immediate and replay deterministic. |

**Art-direction gap:** the opening menu uses a detailed cinematic illustration, while course scenery combines photo backdrops with relatively simple geometric foregrounds. The rider/bike are more detailed than many nearby props but occupy a small part of the phone frame. A studio pass must resolve this hierarchy through cohesive authored scenery, object scale, lighting, effects and camera, then prove it in motion across all four biomes. A still frame or a texture-only replacement is insufficient.

**Unmeasured here:** high-tier material quality on the user's iPhone, sustained frame pacing, audible mix and haptics. The screenshot/trailer review in [the App Store audit](../ios-app-store-audit/README.md) is historical media, not current-release sign-off.
