## Why five days of work have not produced a good rider

The task is character modeling, but the working method became constrained mesh
registration and its certification. It assumes generated garment topology is
valuable enough to preserve nearly verbatim, then spends effort solving the
consequences. Source identity, normalized weights, exact transforms and successful
export are useful engineering facts. None makes trousers surround legs, gives a
glove anatomical finger loops, or places a sole beneath a foot.

I directly inspected the three actual `construction02/selected-progress01` PNGs.
The standing front/profile already show extensive exposed hip/leg areas through
the jeans and feet outside the boots. The reach frame also exposes the axilla.
Hands are bare. These are sufficient to reject the current construction without
waiting for a more sophisticated motion test. This is a negative static finding,
not a moving-art score. I did not replay films in this audit.

The denim also reads as heavily crumpled, angular material. The images alone do
not separate bad fit, damaged normals, old compact UV transfer and original donor
shape; do not pretend a clearance solver will fix all four. The intact selected
jeans/glove reference images and original boot lateral render were also viewed.
The glove's pads, stitched panels and articulated finger shape, and the boot's
sole, toe box, laces and short collar are concrete appearance features to retain.

The strongest process failure is explicit in code:

| Evidence | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| `jeans-fit01/register-sections.py:3–6,258,284–293` | Every original point/face must remain; every resulting triangle must pass a bespoke clearance certificate before a qualified garment output. |

Thus **appearance preservation has been incorrectly tightened into topology
preservation**. Even the later source-appearance correction permits a derived
runtime mesh with explicit lineage. It rejects generic substitutes, not proper
retopology of the chosen garments. This audit proposes no permission expansion.

The earlier five-day wardrobe audit already recommended deliberate new jeans,
boot and glove topology. The current restart adopted a better wearer, but then
returned to the same constrained-donor approach. Renaming section/ray/field
methods is not the structural change required by the plan's failure budget.

## The standard route, and what it actually means here

Blender's manual explicitly distinguishes animation retopology from automatic
remeshing: deformation needs deliberately arranged topology. Its own character
training includes shirt/pants retopology with modeled folds and deliberately
avoids dynamic clothing for ease of animation. These are directly relevant
precedents, not a claim that one button will finish this rider.
[Blender retopology](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.0/modeling/meshes/retopology.html),
[Blender Studio shirt/pants workflow](https://studio.blender.org/training/stylized-character-workflow/5e70a5b3db0e8bc0c8b226ec/).

A current production example describes silhouette/fold authoring, sculpt cleanup,
retopology, high-to-low baking, and engine integration as separate stages. That
supports changing topology while retaining appearance. It does not require us
to buy the same software stack.
[Tides of Tomorrow character artist workflow](https://www.marvelousdesigner.com/en/learn/userspotlight/post/9aba618a803e4e5eb7adb3f7ac375ee9).

The practical sequence is **fit the large forms → author clean deformation
surfaces → transfer selected detail/material → skin and correct → play the
whole outfit in the actual engine**. The first material transfer is enough to
show the real chosen look; final texture optimization follows the sound dressed
structure. Neither a gray suit nor flat palette placeholders meet this request.

Use the current coherent wearer and shared75 rig as the construction foundation,
subject to its still-open anatomical motion review. Keep the liked face/hair and
current correct engineering transport. Do not restart anatomy, naming or engines
merely to avoid the garment work. Fit all pieces in that one rest pose and one
master scene, with visible body and neighboring garments throughout authoring.
Keep the selected facial proportions/features, hairline, color and texture while
checking a continuous jaw/throat/neck into the chest. A pin-perfect face on a
rigid bust shelf is still wrong. Retain the existing integrated face if its
moving transition works; repair the neck surface/weights only where it does not.
No facial-animation work is needed for this scope.

### Concrete garment decisions

| Garment | Intended construction now | What must visibly survive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hoodie | Clone selected25. Fit torso and sleeves with an authored low-resolution cage or direct proportional edits, using shoulder/armhole/elbow/cuff landmarks. Rebuild the axilla bridge/armhole and elbow loops wherever existing faces cannot bend. Preserve the usable hood, torso folds and material; patch only what needs new topology, then rebake changed regions. Do not reuse an `abs(X)` torso/sleeve split. | Mustard fabric, selected hood/collar silhouette, volume, hem/cuffs and major folds; no armpit exposure or sleeve tube pinching. |
| Jeans | Use the selected donor as the sculpt/reference in the same scene. Author a continuous pelvis/crotch and two leg shells with waist/cuff boundaries and hip/knee loops. Set waistband, crotch height, knee and ankle stations against this wearer; shape the loose volume and selected folds deliberately. Fit or sculpt the dense reference to this same shape for baking. Remove malformed generated inner/exterior topology from the production derivative rather than solving its cavity homology. | Blue denim, actual fly, pockets, seams, waistband, selected relaxed silhouette and recognizable folds. Large silhouette folds remain geometry; weave/stitch/wear detail can be baked. |
| Gloves | Begin with the established anatomical hand topology as a construction guide, create a separate glove shell, and deliberately reshape it to the selected padded silhouette. Give thumb/webs and each finger usable bend loops, ease and a cuff lip. Copy/sculpt the selected knuckle/palm pads and strap as retained or rebuilt surface parts; transfer the selected black leather/stitch detail. A raw uniformly inflated body hand is only a starting scaffold and cannot be the delivered glove. | Five shaped fingers, thumb opposition, stitched panels, knuckle padding and actual cuff/strap. No inflated mitten, smooth black hand or bare hands. |
| Boots | Fit one boot against heel, plantar plane, toe tip/width and ankle landmarks, not total bounds. Keep useful donor exterior parts; retopologize/rebuild toe box, ankle opening and sole where required. Author a short inner collar only as far as cameras can see. Shape the whole outer boot to enclose the full unchanged foot. Give the sole/heel/most upper predominantly foot-bone attachment, with deliberate limited toe/ankle transitions where needed. | Selected compact profile, outsole thickness, heel, toe box, lace/tongue/collar and black material; no toes through the sole and no rubbery bending of the rigid sole. |

The jeans' lower cuff is authored over the actual boot collar in neutral and
flexion; do not independently finish the leg and later stretch it to cover the
shoe. Each glove uses the same75-bone hand chains as the skin, including thumb
opposition, and is judged around the finite real handlebar grip. The boot's short
collar must articulate relative to the shin without dragging the whole outsole;
toe flex, if used, occurs around the actual ball joint in a limited forefoot zone.

An authored deformation cage is a way to move large forms coherently, not another
automatic anatomy detector. Blender already implements Mesh Deform; the cage
must enclose its subject and its binding has known cost/limitations. Use simple
low-resolution cages and bounded runs. If a source cannot survive ordinary fit,
replace the bad production surface and reproject selected detail; do not write
another generalized warp. [Blender Mesh Deform](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/mesh_deform.html).

Boots and gloves need a convincing shell surrounding the wearer. They do not
require a fabrication-ready fully modeled internal lining or every original
generated interior triangle. Preserve the complete body; no deleted legs, hidden
feet or alpha masks to camouflage fit failures. Deliberately overlapping laces,
sewn pads and layered hems are classified construction, not automatically a
garment-wide failure.

### Preserve appearance through an ordinary bake

Use new coherent runtime UVs. Keep the dense original corner UVs/materials on the
aligned high-resolution donor; bake base color, roughness/metallic and tangent
normal detail onto the new mesh. Isolate left/right legs, finger panels, sole and
upper as needed so rays cannot pick adjacent unrelated surfaces. Use explicit
bake cages, inspect misses/seams and retain source/derivative pins. Blender's
Selected to Active bake supports this, including manual cages and seam margins.
This preserves traceable selected appearance without requiring one original
triangle ID per final triangle.
[Blender bake workflow](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.4/render/cycles/baking.html).

Normal maps cannot preserve silhouette, parallax of deep folds, holes or separate
laces. Retain/model that geometry. Conversely, keeping millions of triangles or
invalid inherited prototype UVs is not a guarantee of fidelity. Judge the first
textured derived surface against the actual selected source before multiplying
the process across the outfit. Use existing paired dense/material preparation
where it works; do not create a new bake framework as a prerequisite.

### Skinning and corrections

Initialize weights from the compatible body by region, then author them on the
garment. Blender Data Transfer supports interpolated vertex-group transfer;
its proximity mapping is an initialization tool, not anatomical semantics.
Restrict transfers to the correct limb/digit and paint the joint transitions.
Hood/torso use head/neck/spine as appropriate; hem should not accidentally follow
thighs. Jeans legs follow their own thigh/shin; glove digits their own segment
chain; boot soles should not inherit arbitrary skin weights from nearby toes.
[Blender Data Transfer](https://docs.blender.org/manual/id/5.0/modeling/modifiers/modify/data_transfer.html).

For genuine residual joint collapse after correct topology/weights, sculpt a
small number of pose correctives. Blender documents corrective shape keys for
joint intersections. glTF carries morph targets/animation, but arbitrary Blender
drivers do not become a procedural runtime driver system: engine-driven bike
poses must explicitly evaluate the same corrective weights, or consume supported
helper-bone deformation. Test one corrective through the existing private engine
path before relying on it. No cloth simulation is required for this first result.
[Corrective shapes](https://docs.staging.blender.org/manual/en/latest/animation/drivers/workflow_examples.html),
[glTF export](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/5.1/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html).

## The short acceptance loop

1. Save one editable full-outfit candidate with real selected materials. Check
   front/profile/back rest fit and source appearance immediately. Any gross
   exposure, shredded denim, malformed finger or missing garment fails here.
2. Play neutral → reach → crouch → standing ride → seated ride → return, with
   full rider and hand/hip/shoulder close views. Correct observed topology/weight
   defects directly. A good rest render cannot accept movement.
3. Export that same candidate through the already prepared private adapter and
   play actual Garage orbit/idle, both riding extremes, crash and restart. Parent
   records separate face and dressed-body judgments under game lighting. Keep
   the complete body visible in diagnostic controls and show all selected pieces.
4. Run existing transport/reset/contact checks on this candidate. Diagnose actual
   visible or support failures; report collision witnesses by garment part and
   severity. Complete the full active-plan/device/release gates before shipping.

The 2.5/3.5 mm universal padding, all-original-face survival, global strain bounds
relative to malformed donor tessellation and exhaustive interior certificates
are self-imposed solver conditions, not the user's appearance requirement.
Different garments need different ease; fitted leather and loose denim should
not share one magic offset. Retain useful collision tests and the plan's exact
native/GLB/GPU parity requirements. Stop using solver certificates as the entrance
fee to seeing a fully dressed result. This is not permission to silently waive a
failed production test or promote an unqualified candidate.

**Halt criteria:** one authored pass and at most one targeted repair of the same
mechanism. If the shell still cannot fit/bend, replace that region's topology;
do not rename the solver and repeat. If the appearance transfer loses selected
identity, fix the sculpt/bake before assembling more substitutes. If a corrective
works only in Blender, stop its adoption until the engine consumes it. If the
agents cannot author the required topology/art, say that plainly and provide the
existing exact wearer/donors to a character artist; do not spend another day
presenting numerical preparation as clothing production.

## Salvage and retire

Keep immutable originals, selected face/hair/hoodie25, dense PBR/corner UV sources,
the coherent native02 wearer/shared75 rig, repaired hand controls, source/license
records, deterministic engine/contact/export infrastructure and diagnostic
witnesses. Retain all failed experiments as historical evidence.

Retire the pending chart/ARAP/cavity campaigns as the next production work, the
assumption every compact donor face is sacred, and isolated qualification work
that delays a visible complete garment. No new generative model, purchased
wardrobe, generic body-suit substitution or facial-animation campaign is needed.

This is a technically supported route recommendation, not evidence that a new
garment has been built or will reach 9/10 automatically. The missing work is
deliberate character art. Only the actual dressed Garage/game result can close it.
