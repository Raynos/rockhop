# B miniature: asset path to concept fidelity

The played orbit proves the layout and interaction. The direct comparison in `reference-vs-final.jpg` shows that more procedural scatter will not close the quality gap. B's visual weight comes from a few large authored silhouettes, dense surface detail, and unified materials. Keep this prototype isolated until those assets meet the reference at 844×390.

A first Blender terrain pass now exists: `prototypes/world-map-b/assets/sculpted-terrain.glb`, generated by `blender/build_terrain.py`. It has a UV-mapped displaced top, textured eroded cliff faces, and quarry cuts. The matched mobile orbit shows an improvement at the front cliff and quarry, but it is still a procedural mesh; it has no hand-sculpted high-frequency source or baked normal/AO detail. The 8.0 MB, roughly 132,000-triangle GLB also needs optimization before iPhone use.

## Highest-return work, in order

1. **Finish the terrain sculpt.** The first Blender GLB proves replacement and UV/material integration. Hand-sculpt the harbor cliff, forest ravine, quarry strata, and alpine face with sharper reference-matched silhouettes. Bake high-frequency normal and ambient-occlusion maps onto a decimated render mesh. The current GLB is displaced noise geometry and remains too smooth or faceted in several regions.
2. **Author three hero kits.** Model a recognizable rusted working ship and lattice crane for the harbor; three reusable fractured cliff modules with warm/cold material variants; and a quarry conveyor, bridge, and pit-wall kit. These shapes carry the reference at phone size. Reuse small sheds, crates, bollards, railings, and ropes as instanced detail rather than one-off draw calls.
3. **Replace foliage with two quality-gated species.** Use a pine and a broadleaf tree with textured branch/leaf cards attached in 3D, several age/lean variants, and baked trunk normal maps. Keep the cleared roadside corridor. Verify from front, side, and rear orbits; the current green spheres and faceted cones are the largest style mismatch after the cliffs.
4. **Give water physical motion.** A coherent low-cost shader needs scrolling normal maps, shallow/deep color variation, and shoreline foam tied to geometry. Keep the shoreline shape and pier positions. Static rings currently look like decoration instead of water.
5. **Unify material response and lighting.** Build a small atlas for rusted metal, rough wood, sandstone, dark cliff, snow, and road; use consistent texel density, roughness and normal response, then tune one warm studio key and cool fill against the B reference. Add subtle contact shadows rather than more tiny polygonal rubble.

## Gate before integration

- Match B's broad silhouettes and board coverage in camera-matched front, side, and rear captures at 844×390. Use a moving orbit clip as the primary judgment; static images can guide asset iteration.
- Show the twelve real 3D roadside towers and a continuous readable road in landscape. All twelve must remain independently selectable after orbit and focus/close/reset.
- Measure on a physical iPhone in landscape: median and low-frame-rate performance during orbit, texture memory, cold and cached scene readiness, and portrait rotation prompt. Current headless SwiftShader timing is not a device result.
- Preserve the game loading budget by shipping compressed GLB meshes, KTX2 textures, and LOD/instancing for repeated trees, rocks, props, and flags. Audit draw calls before replacing production UI.
