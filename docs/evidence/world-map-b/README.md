# Handcrafted tabletop prototype evidence

- [`orbit-showcase.mp4`](orbit-showcase.mp4) records an actual pointer drag, click on Stage 08, selected-tower focus, and further rotation at 1280×588. It is silent and runs in headless Chromium with SwiftShader. [`rotation-select.mp4`](rotation-select.mp4) retains the initial blank renderer frames for boot inspection.
- [`orbit-contact-sheet.jpg`](orbit-contact-sheet.jpg) samples the played orbit and close-up focus.
- [`front-landscape.png`](front-landscape.png), [`rotated-landscape.png`](rotated-landscape.png), and [`selected-stage.png`](selected-stage.png) show the 3D scene at two camera angles and a selected tower.
- [`mobile-landscape.png`](mobile-landscape.png) and [`mobile-selected.png`](mobile-selected.png) show the 844×390 landscape view. [`portrait-rotate.png`](portrait-rotate.png) verifies the landscape prompt.
- [`capture.json`](capture.json) records errors, render readiness, and a pointer click pass through all twelve towers at 844×390. The click sequence selected stages 1 through 12 in order.
- [`terrain-before-after-orbit.mp4`](terrain-before-after-orbit.mp4) is a synchronized 844×390 comparison: left is the old procedural terrain, right is the Blender GLB. Both sides receive the same played pointer orbit and Stage 08 click. [`terrain-before-after-mobile.jpg`](terrain-before-after-mobile.jpg) shows the matching initial landscape frames, while [`terrain-comparison.json`](terrain-comparison.json) records both runs and errors.
- [`pass0-front.png`](pass0-front.png), [`pass8-art.png`](pass8-art.png), [`pass11-art.png`](pass11-art.png), and [`pass13-art.png`](pass13-art.png) show the visual progression from sparse first pass to the wider sculpted board. [`reference-vs-final.jpg`](reference-vs-final.jpg) directly compares the concept reference with the prototype. [`assets/design/worldmap-3d/mockups/map-styles/B/01.jpg`](../../../assets/design/worldmap-3d/mockups/map-styles/B/01.jpg) is the target reference.

The road, sea, mountains, snow, buildings, and flag towers rotate together with coherent parallax. The screenshot of the concept board is not used as a sprite or surface texture. The Blender tray improves front cliff depth and quarry strata in the matched mobile orbit; the broad silhouette and map interactions are unchanged. The result is still short of B's photographic miniature quality: tree crowns and rock faces remain angular, the ship lacks full industrial detail, water movement is minimal, and mobile GPU performance remains unmeasured. The latest 5.4 second readiness number is headless SwiftShader on this machine and does not predict an iPhone load time. [`NEXT-ASSET-PASS.md`](NEXT-ASSET-PASS.md) records the remaining path to reference fidelity. This standalone prototype is exploratory and is not promoted to the production map.
