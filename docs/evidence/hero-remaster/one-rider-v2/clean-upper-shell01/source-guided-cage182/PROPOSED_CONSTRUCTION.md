Status: no mesh or output geometry. Awaiting the parent's frozen
source-boundaries181 contract and explicit machine checkpoint.

The new cage should be a complete continuous torso/shoulder/axilla/sleeve
lattice. Measured source cross-section contours guide actual front/back depth,
lateral breadth and shoulder slope. Torso rings should preserve the preferred
source's chest-to-hem proportions instead of interpolating an ellipsoid.
Source-based arm sections should establish upper-arm and forearm shape/ease,
with the axilla explicitly checked as a connected garment region and a real
arm/torso gap. These are whole new controls, not displaced retired meshes.

A deliberate side window in the torso/shoulder lattice supplies one indexed
armhole boundary. Shoulder, axilla and proximal sleeve quads reuse that same
physical boundary and share rational edge splits through a registry; sleeve
continuation is authored as part of this cage. No separate transported tube,
frozen concave root, primitive union, remesh or global coordinate weld.
Exceptional valence belongs at shoulder/axilla poles, away from cuffs and the
visible collar edge. Quad diagonals and eventual float32 degeneracy must be
checked before export.

require declared local quad-dominant transition topology. Well-shaped
localized triangle/pole transitions are allowed by the parent; pure quads
are not required at the expense of boundary fidelity. Likewise a 307-node hood-body
boundary cannot automatically become a collar ring. A collar with branches
or multiple loops must be classified first. Possible explicit edge splits
on a proven donor boundary could make even ring counts while preserving its
piecewise linear geometry; that changes donor indices and requires measured
attribute interpolation, not an exact-primitive claim. Alternatively the
quad topology can route parity between odd collar and left cuff using planned
valence poles. The parent selects a supported contract; no implicit fallback. Source nodes
will not be moved, skipped or collapsed to make counts match.
