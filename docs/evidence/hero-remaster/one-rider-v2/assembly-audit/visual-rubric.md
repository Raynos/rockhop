# Matched visual quality gate — ask239

The parent independently rates full body and face closeup against the approved
same-identity mockups. Minimum7/10 EACH; target8/10 EACH. Never average a weak
face into a body pass. Scores describe visual judgment, not automated metrics.

Compare source and actual exported/reimported output side by side. Record
asset/reference SHAs, camera/yaw, framing, neutral light, resolution, pose and
material settings. Crop blank margins only, never warp, retouch or substitute
mockup pixels into the actual render. Disclose unavoidable reference camera
and lighting uncertainty. An unmatched diagnostic score cannot accept a gate.

| Full-body criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Natural proportions and silhouette matching the target | 0–2 |
| Hands/wrists, legs/knees and shoes read as intact anatomy | 0–2 |
| Hoodie/denim/glove/shoe construction and coherent detail | 0–2 |
| Neck integration, rear/sides and absence of visible assembly defects | 0–2 |
| Texture/PBR clarity, smooth surfaces and consistent character identity | 0–2 |

| Face-closeup criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Identity, natural facial proportions and expression | 0–2 |
| Eyes/lids, nose, lips and ears have convincing geometry | 0–2 |
| Brows, buzz hair and facial hair follow the target cleanly | 0–2 |
| Skin/eye/hair textures, normals and material response | 0–2 |
| Profile/rear/three-quarter continuity and neck integration | 0–2 |

Score each criterion from0 absent/broken through1 approximate to2 convincing.
Record points, concrete visible defects, parent verdict and next one-defect
correction. A single attractive front view cannot pass; require the nine-angle
body comparison, face closeups, gray diagnostics, turntable and neck turn/bend.
Missing views or motion report unevaluated, never passed. Exposed holes,
broken anatomy or incompatible skin seams fail regardless of the total.

Below7 in either review, autonomously regenerate with an installed generator
or sculpt/retopologize/edit the affected component. Preserve untouched sources
and frozen comparisons; record actual provenance and failures. Five failed
attempts of an approach trigger a material method change; fifteen unresolved
defect failures retire that repair lineage. A score7–7.9 requires a bounded
target8 refinement attempt, with final limitations explicit. Appearance only
opens checkpoint2; it never implies rig, physics or gameplay readiness.
