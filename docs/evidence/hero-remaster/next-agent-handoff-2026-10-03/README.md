## How to read the catalogue

**Baseline** means a retained comparison or starting component, not acceptance.
**Reusable technique** means a bounded result whose exact assumptions must hold.
**Failed lesson** means stop repeating its parameter family without a new
mechanism or domain. **Superseded** retains history but loses priority.
**Unverified** has not passed the named visual/motion gate. **N/R** means no
recorded numerical score. Scores are subjective parent appearance judgments in
specified conditions; do not average body and face, infer a rig score from a
contact test, or invent scores for unreviewed cloud/fallback work.
