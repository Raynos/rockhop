skin, model or render has executed. The finite cavity11 result closes that narrow
diagnostic question; further isolated homology or ray campaigns are not proposed.

each phalanx; tip closure and MCP/PIP/DIP/knuckle cross-sections use actual target
geometry. Keep stable point IDs and complete source/corner ancestry for later
construction. No garment geometry is authored in this first inventory unit.

## Construction contract

Build an anatomical inner surface around the complete target hand, with explicit
offset clearance in wearer metres and finite local curvature/web constraints.
Add a separate outer layer preserving the selected glove's padded dorsal/finger
silhouette, then connect their cuff loops with a real rim. All five finger
interiors, web seams, palm and wrist remain part of the same intended cavity.
Offset vertex normals alone do not prove inside/outside or adequate web space;
normal-offset self-intersections must not be accepted as enclosure.

The core inner and outer glove charts must have matched, stable triangulation
and no unintended caps across the wrist or finger interiors. A mathematically
temporary wrist closure may be used for a declared inside/outside assay, recorded
separately from actual garment faces. It is never added as a hidden production
blocker. Any legitimate source strap/detail would be preserved through an
explicit reviewed feature construction, not a topology-driven deletion.

Skin the new surface in the wearer's own joint frames. Every phalanx row has
source FULL and conditioned FOUR lineage, with mixed fields across real joint
seams. Original bones, rest frames and body fields remain unchanged. New garment
vertices may interpolate the approved hand surface fields; nearest-bone naming
and the previous ambient RBF sweep are not an accepted binding method.

## Required complete enclosure and movement evidence

with signed inside/outside and finite clearance witnesses, thickness, proper
crossings, degenerates, wrist aperture and web coverage. Query complete surface
and declared finite volume/tube coverage; 17 clear zero-radius paths do not prove
a hand-sized cavity. Declare the wearer-metre clearance/thickness targets before
the check and report the actual minima, including failure witnesses.

Run the same exact FULL/FOUR source controls in neutral, spread, curl/fist,
opposition, handle-grip, release and the relevant bike arm/lean poses. Check all
15 finger segments, palm/web/cuff surfaces and unchanged-body contact, including
between-frame or bounded swept coverage where claimed. Export then compare native
and actual engine deformation/material/normal behavior. Parent judges played
moving clips, including grip closure and release; device cost and appearance are
