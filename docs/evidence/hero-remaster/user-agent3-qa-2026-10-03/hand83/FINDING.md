prerequisite** before another grip-pose trial. Within the existing editable glove
geometry and admitted32 wrist/finger columns, an authorized builder should first
identify five geometric finger regions on each hand and match them to the existing

records do not certify five usable fingers, preserved-field anatomical fit,

expansion or candidate is justified by this audit. Moving comparisons must remain
