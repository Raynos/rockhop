# C island harbor model pass

**Play the silent capture:** [original orbit](before/played-orbit.mp4) · [detailed orbit before batching](after/played-orbit.mp4) · [optimized detailed orbit](optimized/played-orbit.mp4). All clips enter through Menu → Play, drag the island around, and tap all twelve towers. At the 852×393 landscape viewport, compare [front original](before/front.png) / [front optimized](optimized/front.png), [three-quarter original](before/three-quarter.png) / [three-quarter optimized](optimized/three-quarter.png), and [reverse original](before/reverse.png) / [reverse optimized](optimized/reverse.png). The intermediate [front](after/front.png), [three-quarter](after/three-quarter.png), and [reverse](after/reverse.png) frames show that baking preserved the fuller model silhouettes. The source composition is the [selected C-island image](../orbit-round4/selected-reference.jpg).

The previous harbor silhouette had a flat rectangular deck, box cabin, thin single-plane crane, and individual cuboid pier planks. The new trawler has a seven-station lofted steel hull with rising bow sheer and chine, a tapered open deck, paired gunwales and guard rails, a sloped red pilot-house roof with glazing, a funnel, fenders, and mast stays. The crane now has four tapered legs, braces on both sides, a triangular lattice boom, counterweight, support stays, hoist cable, block, and hook. The dock uses timber boards and piles, with two mooring cleats. Thin structural rods are merged by material, then the static harbor is baked into one vertex-painted mesh. This replaces many separate draw calls while keeping the modeled shapes and their colors. It does flatten per-part roughness and metalness to one harbor finish; at the reviewed phone framing this does not visibly change the model, but close zoom fidelity remains a tradeoff.

This is a stylized low-poly interpretation of the reference. The image is a full island scene, so it does not resolve the boat's hidden side or exact crane construction. The modeled front view now reads as a smaller working boat beside an open industrial crane. From the reverse orbit the crane still has depth and the boat keeps its pilot-house and tapered hull silhouette. The harbor remains an environmental accent; it does not cover level 01–03 towers or change hit targets. Additional wear and rigging would not be legible at this phone framing and were left out.
