# C-map water plane probe — negative art finding

The [selected island reference](../orbit-round4/selected-reference.jpg) has an open sea without the regular grid and dark rectangular patch visible in the playable map's oblique orbit. I first tried stronger wear in the road mesh; its phone-size result was effectively invisible, so I discarded that change. This probe instead removes the water mesh's 6.25 m vertex-wave grid, leaving its animated fragment ripples, foam and horizon fade. The C map remains review-only.

The [before](before/played-orbit.webm) and [after](after/played-orbit.webm) clips are silent headless plays of the real map selector at **852×393 landscape, DPR 1**. Each opens Menu → Play, touch-drags through the front, oblique and reverse angles, and taps all 12 towers in order. The [before](before/measurements.json) and [after](after/measurements.json) reports contain the camera samples, selected course IDs and page errors. Representative [front](before/front.png) / [after front](after/front.png), [oblique](before/orbit-1.png) / [after oblique](after/orbit-1.png), and [reverse](before/orbit-3.png) / [after reverse](after/orbit-3.png) frames come from those plays.

The flattened plane reduces rendered geometry from **412,634 to 399,836 triangles** in the front and oblique frames, a reduction of **12,798**; draw calls remain **256**. Both recorded orbits sample about **5–6 fps** after the first frame in headless SwiftShader, so this is not a demonstrated pacing gain. Both passes select **12/12 correct towers** with **zero page errors**. `node --check`, `pnpm typecheck` and `pnpm lint` passed for the candidate.

**Judgment: reject as a visual fidelity round.** The dark water patch and regular-looking lines remain at phone size, while the pixel difference in the oblique frame is minimal. Removing the vertex waves buys geometry but does not close the selected-reference gap; a separate no-video benchmark and physical landscape iPhone measurement would be needed to judge any performance benefit. The source experiment is left uncommitted for the parent to revert or carry into a later performance round. Nothing changes the route, biomes, towers or default painted selector.

Reproduce using `node docs/evidence/world-map-c/next-art-round/capture.mjs ../water-plane-probe/<label> 5184 1 --taps --viewport=852x393` against the round-three Vite config, with `<label>` as `before` or `after` at the respective source state.
