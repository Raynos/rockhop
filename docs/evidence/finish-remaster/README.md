## Findings and remaining failures

The result now spends its limited space on one earned medal, time, PB relation, faults, a next riding goal, and four labeled actions. First clear, a faster PB, a medal upgrade, and a repeat clear are visually and verbally distinct. Scrap earned and wallet are dashes with a pending note, so the prototype does not invent a reward ledger.

The prototype hero uses an enlarged gameplay frame, and its rider is soft; the checkered finish is outside the tighter crop. Production needed an authored camera move and a live reward ledger, which the integration below now supplies as a full-width crossing beat followed by a rider close shot. The prototype clip itself proves interface transitions only, not restart latency, live destinations, deterministic replay or rewards. Physical iPhone touch and safe-area testing remain open.

The video is 852×394 because H.264 pads the odd CSS height. `ffprobe` reports one video stream and no audio stream.

The authored C1 Rookie recording is manually stepped at 120 Hz, six input ticks per 20 fps video frame, while the App's display clock is paused. It reaches the byte-identical reference hash `2bfe061963ffb058`, 0:30.350, zero faults, a real Diamond first finish, +300 Scrap, and a 300 wallet that appears again in Garage. The countdown was filmed from the loaded course; its last fraction was skipped to arm exact replay. The machine record shows 0 page errors. This proves the rendered App route and recorded ride under deterministic automation; it does not measure a stranger's attempts, real-time play pacing, or physical iPhone touch and safe areas. Headless ANGLE SwiftShader used the game's low-quality rendering tier.

The first continuous capture was rejected even though its physics hash and reward passed: it reloaded C1 under a paused animation clock, leaving the renderer in its entry placeholder, so most of the ride was flat blue biome fog. That misleading clip was deleted. The accepted recapture keeps the original loaded course through countdown and ride; moving frames show terrain, bike, obstacles, and scenery throughout. The finish and Garage frames still show a tiny red performance readout near the right edge, which is a visible polish defect in this build.
