## Finished result

One recognizable adult rider with the liked buzz-cut head, mustard hoodie,
blue jeans, black gloves and compact boots. The same assembled person must look
convincing in the actual Garage, stand forward on the pegs, shift back into a
supported low seated pose, and move continuously between them in both directions.
Lean, compression, hop, landing, crash release and instant restart must preserve
identity, body shape, clothing coverage and believable contact on Rookie and Pro.

Face and full-body appearance each retain the separate9/10 target. Judge moving
front/rear/both profiles/three-quarter gray and PBR evidence with complete limbs
visible, then normal gameplay. Source orbits alone cannot qualify animation.
Keep the current playable models until the assembled candidate passes promotion
gates. New bike art, hairstyles/outfits, another backend/model comparison campaign,
full-body cloth, extra generic galleries and physics-engine migration are deferred.

question that blocks the current candidate. Successful metrics must correspond
to the moving visual improvement and exact consumed asset they claim.

as source-ready, assembled, native-motion-qualified, engine-qualified or shipped.
