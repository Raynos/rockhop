# ROCKHOP game remaster — ranked top 20

**Status:** active, gameplay audit in progress. This replaces the 2026-09-28 store-first top 10 after the user played all 12 courses and reported that holding GO can clear them, the bikes lack purpose, and the finish screen and replay loop feel unfinished. The user wants a studio-quality game before store submission. The detailed native checklist remains in [IOS_APP_STORE_FINISH.md](IOS_APP_STORE_FINISH.md) and [STORE_RELEASE.md](STORE_RELEASE.md).

## The game a studio would build

ROCKHOP is a compact, premium-feeling trials campaign: every course has a legible riding problem, a deliberate input solution, a fast crash/retry loop, and a finish worth chasing again. The opening teaches brake, throttle release, weight shift, preload and landing through real but understandable obstacles. Later courses combine techniques. Difficulty labels follow measured player experience; the user's Hard-to-Super-Extreme suggestion was an over-correction, not a fixed four-zone naming rule. The two bikes create different valid lines and the earned second bike opens the highest achievement on the last four stages. The world map, course scenery, rider, bike, camera, sound and rewards share one art direction. The player should want another attempt because they understand what to improve.

**Current evidence:** the user's iPhone playthrough reports all 12 feel too easy and can be completed by holding the green GO control. A [deterministic default-seed probe](../evidence/gameplay-audit/README.md) with throttle=1 and all other controls neutral for 600 simulated seconds clears 8/12 on Rookie and 6/12 on Pro; baseline C1 Rookie reaches the top medal in 31.025 seconds with zero faults. Some fixed-input runs crash repeatedly and never clear under this protocol, so the probe does not reproduce the user's exact 12/12 report. It does prove the central design failure. Baseline C1 explicitly set a first-try held-gas goal; the [C1 integration branch](../evidence/c1-production-integration/README.md) replaces that with a braking problem and remains under qualification. The [registered C2 branch change](../evidence/c2-production-integration/README.md) makes held GO fail and a corrected crane landing clear both bikes with exact replay; it needs touch learners and cleaner landing art before release. The bike audit finds that Pro is selectable immediately, shares Rookie tyre grip and travel, and Rookie goldens already earn the top medal on D3/S1/S2/S3; see [campaign brief](COURSE_AND_PROGRESSION_REDESIGN.md). The committed `main` result ticket still lacks earned currency and its top medal says “Obsidian.” On the qualification branch, commit `487db605` adds [one-time Scrap rewards, an 800-Scrap Pro purchase, and a player-facing Diamond label](../evidence/scrap-loop/README.md); the finish hero shot and course challenge remain open.

The qualification branch now has [all four final-course upper Diamond routes](../evidence/final-four-diamond/README.md) with exact Node/browser Pro replays and clean Starter lower Gold finishes. These are sampled bike advantages, not final human difficulty proof. [S3's replacement Starter replay](../evidence/s3-fairness/README.md) visibly passes beneath the shelf for Gold while Pro rides above it for Diamond. [A2 Pro](../evidence/a2-pro-clear/README.md) has a clean zero-fault exact reference. The [C island integration review](../evidence/world-map-c/integrated-review/README.md) selects real C1–S3 courses at all 12 towers. Its [latest played art orbit](../evidence/world-map-c/next-art-round/README.md) sharpens snow peaks and cliffs but remains dev-only at 4–5 fps on headless SwiftShader and below the selected art reference. [Cached-start isolation](../evidence/cached-boot/README.md) found 3.3–3.6 s after first script in the harness; an extra 5–7 s delayed even a static page, so no phone startup claim follows. The [latest four-section gate](../evidence/round-gate-2026-09-28/README.md) passed exact clear, crash and one-tick restart logic but missed first-frame and restart-frame timing on SwiftShader; the remaster remains off `main`.

The [twelve-course challenge round](../evidence/campaign-retarget/README.md) moved the fixed-input result from 8/12 Rookie and 6/12 Pro passive clears to **0/72 in 600-second three-seed runs**. A [final-four clock round](../evidence/late-medal-clock-2026-09-28/README.md) now gives pinned clean Pro upper routes 0.25–0.62 s to spare for Diamond while the distinct Starter lower routes remain Gold; player times must calibrate fairness. Played clips and exact replays cover the new Alpine, Quarry, Snowline S1 and Coast C3 gates; [23 available campaign bot goldens](../evidence/final-four-diamond/README.md) were re-proved exactly against the current browser build. This is the first mechanical pass: several courses still clear in one expert-bot attempt, new phone players have not measured learnability, medal clocks remain provisional, and sampled route advantages do not establish a final bike requirement. The earlier D3 upper-path experiment was removed after Rookie succeeded more often in its sweep; the new D3 geometry has a separate sampled Pro advantage.

## Small progression proposal to test

- **Medal ladder:** Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond. Underlying `platinum` key remains until a tested save migration is justified; the display and art change to Diamond.
- **Scrap rewards:** first Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond on each course makes its total lifetime payout 100/160/220/300 Scrap. Upgrading a medal grants only the difference; replaying the same medal grants zero. This makes skill rewarding without a grinding loophole.
- **Bike two price:** 800 Scrap. Eight Bronze medals pay exactly 800, so every player who reaches the last four can buy it. No other paid item at launch and no real-money purchase.
- **Bike roles:** Starter is light, precise and forgiving; the second bike has stronger torque, more travel and grip for late climbs/landings, with more inertia and harder tight balance. The final four Diamond lines require its measured capability, not a hard-coded medal denial.
- **Result flow:** show the medal, technique and rewards from the just-finished ride; explain the next reachable goal; then offer immediate Retry, Next, Map and Replay.

The currency/purchase loop, Diamond UI and four optional final-course routes are implemented on the qualification branch, not production; bike capability proof, readable route cues and twelve-course human difficulty remain design and engineering work. Store submission follows gameplay quality.

**Finish study:** a [played landscape prototype](../evidence/finish-remaster/README.md) tests the four truthful result states and full-size Retry/Next/Map/Replay actions. It improves the layout direction, but the rider shot is soft and the routes and Scrap display are placeholders. The qualification branch now has the reward ledger on the real result ticket; the hero shot, richer finish states and twelve-course replay goals still need priorities 8–9.
