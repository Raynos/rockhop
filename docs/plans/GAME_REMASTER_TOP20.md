# ROCKHOP game remaster — ranked top 20

**Status:** active, gameplay audit in progress. This replaces the 2026-09-28 store-first top 10 after the user played all 12 courses and reported that holding GO can clear them, the bikes lack purpose, and the finish screen and replay loop feel unfinished. The user wants a studio-quality game before store submission. The detailed native checklist remains in [IOS_APP_STORE_FINISH.md](IOS_APP_STORE_FINISH.md) and [STORE_RELEASE.md](STORE_RELEASE.md).

## The game a studio would build

ROCKHOP is a compact, premium-feeling trials campaign: every course has a legible riding problem, a deliberate input solution, a fast crash/retry loop, and a finish worth chasing again. A short practice run teaches brake, release throttle, shift weight, preload and land. The 12 campaign courses are three **Hard** Coast, three **Very Hard** Alpine, three **Extreme** Quarry and three **Super Extreme** Snowline stages. They combine techniques rather than calling an easy drive-through a level. The two bikes create different valid lines and the earned second bike opens the highest achievement on the last four stages. The world map, course scenery, rider, bike, camera, sound and rewards share one art direction. The player should want another attempt because they understand what to improve.

**Current evidence:** the user's iPhone playthrough reports all 12 feel too easy and can be completed by holding the green GO control. A deterministic default-seed probe with throttle=1 and all other controls neutral for 240 simulated seconds clears 8/12 on Rookie and 6/12 on Pro; C1 Rookie reaches the top medal in 31.025 seconds with zero faults. Some fixed-input runs crash repeatedly and never clear under this protocol, so the probe does not reproduce the user's exact 12/12 report. It does prove the central design failure. C1 source explicitly sets a first-try held-gas goal. The existing result ticket shows time, bails and medal but gives no earned currency, upgrade progress or strong finish celebration. The present top medal is labelled “Obsidian”; the user selected **Diamond**.

## Small progression proposal to test

- **Medal ladder:** Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond. Underlying `platinum` key remains until a tested save migration is justified; the display and art change to Diamond.
- **Scrap rewards:** first Bronze/Silver/Gold/Diamond on each course makes its total lifetime payout 100/160/220/300 Scrap. Upgrading a medal grants only the difference; replaying the same medal grants zero. This makes skill rewarding without a grinding loophole.
- **Bike two price:** 800 Scrap. Eight Bronze medals pay exactly 800, so every player who reaches the last four can buy it. No other paid item at launch and no real-money purchase.
- **Bike roles:** Starter is light, precise and forgiving; the second bike has stronger torque, more travel and grip for late climbs/landings, with more inertia and harder tight balance. The final four Diamond lines require its measured capability, not a hard-coded medal denial.
- **Result flow:** show the medal, technique and rewards from the just-finished ride; explain the next reachable goal; then offer immediate Retry, Next, Map and Replay.

This is a design candidate, not shipped logic. A C1 vertical slice and two-bike measurements must validate it before it is propagated to all 12 levels. Store submission work remains active but follows gameplay quality.
