## Outcome and scope

Deliver ONE white adult rider with the liked face, buzz cut, mustard hoodie,
blue denim, gloves and shoes. It must look good standing from every review angle,
in the actual Garage, and through the two essential riding motions: forward /
standing on the pegs, and back / low seated weight shift. Include the transitions
between them. The person, face and clothing must remain recognizable in engine.

Baseline quality comes before more costumes, alternate hairstyles, helmets,
new bike art, unrelated generator searches or finish reactions. Bounded local
model comparisons under the canonical fitting contract may proceed. Preserve shipped
outfits and bike choices; this plan does not remaster their models. The existing
Rookie bike is the first fixed fitting fixture; the same rider must subsequently
work with both real bikes. A later hairstyle can replace the buzz cut after the
baseline face, geometry, rig and runtime pass. No date or commit count substitutes
for a passed milestone; current accepted milestones are **0/6**.

## Why the approach changes

The catalogue contains many local numeric improvements that never became a
convincing whole character. A favourable strain score, closed seam, new skeleton
or grip point has repeatedly coexisted with poor silhouette or unsupported sitting.
Source-preservation bounds also constrained geometry that needed reconstruction.
This plan stops the unbounded patch loop and makes the final pictures and motions
the unit of progress. The existing [attempt catalogue](../evidence/hero-remaster/next-agent-handoff-2026-10-03/README.md)
is mandatory before admitting a new mechanism; old failure counts carry forward.

There is one selected source lineage, one production construction, one bind,
one pose owner and one current candidate. Keep sources immutable as references;
derived production topology may change deliberately to solve construction. It
must retain the liked face, hood and body silhouette in matched visual comparisons.
Do not require every new production vertex to equal a bad source vertex, and do
not silently swap identity or overwrite the original source. Store ancestry and
transfer/bake receipts. Historical structural fallback candidates are controls,
not automatically the new rider because they are newer or manifold.

## Production construction and motion strategy

Treat the liked dense rider as a high-detail reference, then build a deliberate
animation topology and bake/transfer its detail. Start with a reduced prototype
of both core endpoint poses before investing in full PBR or expensive detail.
A fresh T-pose alone cannot decide the viability of the new construction.
The body, jeans and hoodie must fit one canonical geometry and skeleton; three
unrelated generated meshes are not presumed to fit. Generation works from that
same contract alongside anatomical construction. The native stock head is a
fitting control only; preserve the generated head while improving the body.

Before detailed pose polishing, export one early construction and compare the
exact same asset and pose matrices in Blender and the actual engine. Include
rest, true A/T, arms forward, raised/bent elbows, squat and both riding extremes
from front, rear, both profiles and three-quarters. Riding-only correctives do
not qualify a general shoulder/arm repair. Rebuild faulty construction early;
do not resume fused-mesh salvage as the default route.

Define intentional seam and surface ownership at neck, sleeve, waist, glove and
shoe. A clothing layer must have an anatomical purpose, clearance and bend domain;
more overlapping layers are not an improvement by themselves. Prefer one visible
hoodie shell and one visible denim shell with only needed underlying anatomy;
separate exposed skin/head/hands as appropriate. At covered joins, verify no light
leaks or clipping throughout motion. Do not hide a web or hole with double-sided
materials, an extra insert or a normal map. Use explicit thickness only where
visible and useful. Keep the real fingers/palms and shoes as contact surfaces.

Before a reconstruction trial, compare it with the rejected tube, global cloth,
interior-patch and fixed-boundary repair families. Admit it only with a written
new mechanism/domain and an early falsification test on neutral, raised arms
and both riding endpoints. "New topology" without a worked seam/clearance/bend
construction is not a distinct strategy. Sculpt residual silhouette deliberately;
stop generic radius, stiffness, mask or weight sweeps on exhausted architectures.

For each selected bind, pin joint names/order/hierarchy, inverse binds, mesh bind,
units, axes and the current driver. The adapter maps physics states to the rig;
physics remains authoritative and replay invariants unchanged. A change to a bind
or contact trajectory invalidates old corrective/contact proofs. Standalone
choreography first proves motion shape; physics-driven playback must then prove
that the same construction works across the actual game envelope.

At each endpoint, locate the actual saddle, grips and pegs in the engine frame.
Classify forward/standing as supported through pegs/grips with intentional saddle
clearance; classify low seated/back pose by its intended saddle and foot support.
Do not demand saddle contact while deliberately standing. Inspect complete surfaces,
not only joint/socket points. Inspect the rear hip/thigh and inner leg at every
intermediate pose. Build residual pose correctives only after construction/bind
passes, export them, and verify their actual Three.js order/driver. DQ or cloth
features that disappear in the GLB cannot count as runtime repairs.

## Reviews that make progress understandable

Each round delivers one small visual packet under the new evidence directory:

- One overview board: target / previous kept candidate / current candidate at the
  same named views, with unchanged crop/light where possible. Label source and date.
- Full-resolution face and worst-defect views. Include the back/underside, not only
  the best three-quarter image. Keep hole witnesses separate from montage omissions.
- Three primary motion angles: side, front three-quarter and rear three-quarter.
  Show forward → neutral → back → neutral → forward, then reverse and slow playback.
  Continuous motion is played, not inferred from a few posed frames.
- A short decision: what became visibly better, what remains broken, which gate
  is next, and whether the candidate was kept or rejected. Source hashes live in
  a machine-readable manifest rather than burying the picture in technical labels.

The user can give direct picture-by-picture feedback; record it and update targets
or defects. Asking for another direction vote is not the default. Existing delegated
art decisions remain autonomous; only actual device/stranger observations require
those observers. Parent independently judges clips. Full-body and face each must
reach the existing **9/10** target against the frozen references; never average
away a poor face. Motion and contact are separate passes, not aesthetic averages.
Reversible garment reconstruction and iteration proceed within the frozen visual
contract. Do not reopen topology/autonomy votes; ask only about a genuinely
unresolved art choice. Keep the liked head while that choice is pending.

During active construction, deliver actual Blender AND engine videos, multiangle
boards and the remaining visible defects every two hours. Root owns hourly
generation supervision while a job runs. These are reporting duties, not evidence
that an automation exists. The [private first review packet](https://rockhop-rider-baseline-review.raynos.chatgpt.site)
contains retained references/diagnostics; replace or extend it with dated current
candidate packets rather than presenting historical footage as current progress.

Maintain `STATUS.json` with current candidate hash, six gate states and evidence
links; maintain `DEFECTS.md` with view/time witnesses, cause, owner and next proof.
A defect closes only when its original witness plus relevant held-out frames pass.
Do not report percentage from commits, agent effort, number of samples or asset count.
